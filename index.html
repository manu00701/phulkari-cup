📜 Regulations
MATCH RULES
🏏
T10 Tennis Ball Format
Tennis ball cricket, T10 format only. No leather ball permitted.
🔟
10 Overs Per Innings
Each team faces exactly 10 overs. No extensions unless Super Over.
🪙
Toss 15 Min Before Match
Both team captains must be present at the toss 15 minutes prior to scheduled start.
⏰
Late Arrival = Walkover
Teams arriving late forfeit the match. Punctuality is mandatory — no grace time.
🚫
Throw/Chuck = Direct Ban
Any bowler using an illegal throwing action will be immediately banned from the tournament.
⚖️
Umpire Decision Final
All umpire decisions are final and binding. Arguing with umpires may result in dismissal.
➕
Wide & No Ball = Extra + Ball
Every wide or no-ball awards 1 extra run and must be re-bowled. Free hit on no-ball.
💸
Entry Fee Non-Refundable
₹3,799 entry fee is fixed and non-refundable under any circumstances once submitted.
⚡
Super Over on Tie
In case of a tied match, a single Super Over will be played to determine the winner.
⚠️ All players must follow the rules. Violation may lead to disqualification without refund.
